# RIGHTS + ORIGINALITY POLICY

Status: HARD GATE

## Core rule

Import proven ideas, not stolen content.

## Allowed research uses

Third-party public content may be analyzed for:
- trends;
- hooks;
- topics;
- pacing;
- story structure;
- product discovery;
- audience reactions;
- format intelligence.

Research permission does not imply republication permission.

## Publication source classes

Preferred:
1. original footage/assets;
2. commissioned assets;
3. licensed stock/media;
4. public-domain assets with verified status;
5. platform-provided remix/library assets used under their terms;
6. third-party material with verified commercial rights and a production that also satisfies platform originality policy.

## Prohibited default workflow

```text
download third-party video
→ remove watermark
→ minor crop/speed/caption/voice change
→ repost
```

This is not the MAGASIN business model.

## Platform originality gate

Before publishing, verify:
- Is the creative substance meaningfully original?
- Is there original analysis, narrative, education, entertainment or demonstrated transformation?
- Are visual/audio commercial rights available?
- Would a reviewer understand what we created?
- Is the content mass-produced/repetitive?
- Does the platform have a stricter reused/unoriginal rule than copyright law?

## Important distinction

Copyright permission and monetization eligibility are different tests.

Even permitted material can fail a platform's reused/unoriginal-content monetization policy.

## Fail closed

If commercial rights are uncertain, do not publish automatically.

Set:
RIGHTS_STATUS=WAIT_OWNER

The Robot never invents a license or assumes “found online” means reusable.
