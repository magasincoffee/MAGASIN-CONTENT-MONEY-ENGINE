# 2. Executive decision — ONE route only

## SELECTED ROUTE

**Market:** United States  
**Language:** English  
**Distribution Surface:** Pinterest organic discovery / organic Pins  
**Primary Monetization Model:** Affiliate commerce

Canonical route:

```text
US
× English
× Pinterest organic Pins
× Affiliate commerce
```

This is the **only starting route retained on the current first-cash critical path**.

It is not a claim that Pinterest will generate more traffic or a higher conversion rate than YouTube. Those outcomes are **UNKNOWN until measured**.

It is selected because the current evidence shows a shorter and simpler path from a cold-start content asset to a compliant outbound affiliate click:

- Pinterest's normal Pin creation flow includes a destination **Link** field; creators can send people to another website and Pinterest explicitly notes that creators use links to websites they are affiliated with. [S1][S2]
- Pinterest explicitly permits affiliate content, provided it is original, adds unique value, is transparent, and is not spam/manipulative. [S3]
- Pinterest discovery occurs through search and recommendation surfaces; users search for Pins, products, ideas and trends. No follower/view threshold is documented as a prerequisite to create a normal organic Pin with a link. This does **not** guarantee reach. [S1][S4][S5]
- YouTube long-form external links in descriptions/comments can be clickable, but require **Advanced Features**. Access to Advanced Features requires phone verification first, then sufficient channel history or identity verification by ID/video where available. [S6][S7][S8]
- YouTube search itself can surface new videos, but new channels/videos may take several days to appear in search and ranking depends on relevance, engagement and quality. Thus its cold-start organic distribution speed is also not guaranteed. [S9][S10]
- A Pinterest Pin can be produced from an image or video plus title/description/link; a YouTube long-form route necessarily requires a video asset and associated watch-quality burden. Therefore **lower Pinterest production cost/speed is an operational inference**, not a measured MCME fact. [S1][S11]

### Decision confidence

**MEDIUM.**

The policy/prerequisite advantage is verified. The actual organic reach, clicks, conversion and settled cash are not.

# 5. Awin vs impact.com vs Amazon — cold-start acquisition comparison

No approval is assumed.

## 5.1 Awin

**Current evidence**

- Awin accepts publisher applications using a website, social media channels or other promotional methods. [S13]
- Awin says publisher applications are typically processed within about **24 hours**, though they may take longer if information is incomplete or additional checks are needed. [S13]
- After network approval, publisher applications to individual advertisers are separate.
- Some Awin advertisers use **automatic approval**, meaning the publisher is partnered immediately and can create links without waiting for manual advertiser review. [S14]
- Other advertiser approvals can take hours to weeks. [S15]
- Minimum publisher payment threshold is $20 USD / £20 / €20 (or equivalent for other currencies); Awin processes publisher payments twice per month after commissions are confirmed and the payment conditions are met. [S18]
- Actual advertiser commission rate, cookie, validation time, reversal behavior, traffic-source permission and Owner acceptance are **UNKNOWN** until a specific real account/program is examined.

**Cold-start assessment**

**Best first attempt** because it is the only network in this comparison with current official evidence for:
- a typical short publisher review window; and
- explicit advertiser auto-approval paths.

This is not a guarantee of Owner or advertiser approval.

## 5.2 impact.com

**Current evidence**

- impact.com currently invites creators, influencers and publishers to sign up as partners. [S22]
- Its Marketplace lets partners discover brands and apply to partner with brands. [S23]
- Once partnered with a brand, the affiliate can copy a tracking link and promote it. [S24]
- Partner funds become eligible for withdrawal after prerequisites are met and the impact.com balance reaches $10 USD or equivalent. Payment prerequisites can include phone verification, tax/payment requirements and bank/PayPal setup. [S25]
- Brand-specific approval, contract/payment event, locking/validation period, commission and Owner acceptance remain **UNKNOWN**.

**Cold-start assessment**

**Fallback 1.**

impact.com is credible and has strong tracking/payment infrastructure, but the current public evidence used here does not establish an Awin-style advertiser auto-approval path or a comparable typical publisher-review window.

## 5.3 Amazon Associates

**Current evidence**

- Amazon US reviews the application only after at least **3 qualifying sales within the first 180 days**. [S16]
- Amazon expects robust original content and says social-network properties should be established with a substantive number of organic followers/likes, in most cases at least **500**. [S16]
- Amazon's accepted US social-network list currently names Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube, TikTok and Twitch; **Pinterest is not on that list**. [S16]
- Amazon commission payment is approximately **60 days after the end of the month** in which the commission is earned, subject to thresholds and payment/tax setup. [S19]

**Cold-start assessment**

**Fallback 2 — conditional, not a Pinterest-only cold-start assumption.**

Amazon is retained only if, at the time of execution, the Owner has an eligible Amazon-approved property/site and Amazon terms permit the intended Pinterest-to-Amazon flow for that registered property. A Pinterest-only new property must **not** be assumed eligible.


This order is a **provisional acquisition order**, not a claim of approval.

# 9. Invalidation conditions for the selected route

The selected route must be overturned only by evidence, not preference.

**Invalidate US × English × Pinterest organic × Affiliate if any of these occur:**

1. **Surface-policy invalidation** — Pinterest changes policy so the intended disclosed affiliate-link flow is not permitted.
2. **Network/merchant invalidation** — the viable networks/advertisers available to Owner disallow Pinterest/social traffic for the selected niche.
3. **Owner eligibility invalidation** — Awin and impact.com cannot accept the Owner/property/payment configuration without an unresolved Owner-only blocker, and Amazon fallback is not eligible.
4. **Attribution invalidation** — the chosen network/merchant cannot provide a measurable click/action/commission path.
5. **Settlement invalidation** — commissions cannot reach an Owner-authorized payout rail under acceptable legal/payment conditions.
6. **Rights/policy invalidation** — the niche requires rights, claims or regulated-content handling that breaks the low-friction premise.
7. **Real experiment invalidation** — after a bounded, policy-compliant organic test, Pinterest fails to produce measurable qualified outbound traffic while a controlled alternative surface produces materially stronger verified commercial evidence at lower total friction.
8. **Lead-gen challenger invalidation** — a buyer-first lead-generation route is actually secured with explicit acceptance/payment terms and demonstrates shorter validated-to-settled cash with no higher Owner/manual/compliance dependency.
9. **Economics invalidation** — real MCME data shows total content + compliance + payout friction makes the selected route inferior on time-to-settled-cash and risk-adjusted cash contribution.

Until one of these conditions is met, alternatives remain deleted from the current critical path.
