# MONETIZATION STACK V1

## Goal

Every content experiment must declare at least one primary monetization mechanism before production.

## Revenue layers

### M1 — Platform monetization
Examples:
- video ad/revenue share;
- creator rewards;
- fan funding;
- subscriptions;
- platform shopping incentives.

Pros: native.
Cons: eligibility thresholds, policy dependence, country availability.

### M2 — Affiliate commerce
Content → click → merchant → commission.

Metrics:
- CTR;
- EPC;
- conversion rate;
- AOV;
- commission rate;
- refund/cancellation rate;
- settled commission.

### M3 — Lead generation
Content → lead capture/contact/action → qualified lead value.

Use only lawful, transparent collection and consent flows.

### M4 — Sponsorship / brand deals
Requires audience/format proof and brand suitability.

### M5 — Owned digital products
Examples:
- templates;
- guides;
- datasets;
- software;
- memberships;
- courses where expertise/value exists.

### M6 — Owned physical commerce
Only when unit economics and fulfillment are proven.

### M7 — Services
Examples:
- localized video production;
- voice production;
- content research;
- UGC production.

### M8 — IP / licensing
Original media assets, formats or libraries may become licensable assets.

## Monetization portfolio rule

Do not depend on a single platform payout.

Target mature content asset:

```text
Content
 ├── Platform cash
 ├── Affiliate cash
 ├── Lead/Product cash
 └── Sponsor/IP optionality
```

## Money-loop priority

For fastest validation, compare:
1. affiliate commerce;
2. platform-native monetization;
3. lead generation.

Choose based on shortest credible time-to-first-cash for the selected market.
