## 1. Executive conclusion

### Answer to the controlling question

**Which route is currently most capable of reaching first real cash fastest?**

**Affiliate commerce is the strongest first-cash route to test first**, using original, high-intent content distributed on surfaces that do **not** require platform-native monetization eligibility before an outbound link can create an attributable sale.

Why:

1. **Platform-native monetization is gated.** YouTube’s expanded YPP still requires 500 subscribers plus activity/watch thresholds for early monetization features, and ad/Premium revenue requires higher thresholds. TikTok Creator Rewards requires location eligibility, at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the prior 30 days, plus original videos of at least one minute. A brand-new creator therefore cannot assume day-one platform revenue.
2. **Affiliate commission can be generated before those audience thresholds.** Amazon Associates, Awin and impact.com all support tracked partner/affiliate links. Awin documents automatic approval for some advertiser programs; impact.com documents link-based earning after a brand partnership is active.
3. **Affiliate attribution is directly measurable.** Click → merchant transaction → commission gives a cleaner first revenue signal than CPM/RPM estimates.
4. **Affiliate settled cash is not instant.** Amazon states commission income is paid approximately 60 days after month-end; Awin requires confirmed commission and a payout threshold; impact.com requires payout prerequisites and at least a $10 balance. Therefore MCME must distinguish **first attributed revenue** from **first settled cash**.
5. **Lead generation can beat affiliate on value per conversion in some B2B offers, but it usually adds sales-cycle/qualification friction and more Owner/compliance dependency.** It remains a second route worth testing with one narrow B2B referral hypothesis, not the default first route.
6. **Pure repost/reupload is rejected.** YouTube explicitly separates copyright permission from reused-content monetization eligibility; permission alone does not make minimally modified reused content monetizable.

### Current operating hypothesis

The smallest credible money loop is:

```text
HIGH-INTENT QUESTION / PRODUCT PROBLEM
→ ORIGINAL ENGLISH CONTENT
→ DISCOVERY SURFACE
→ DISCLOSED AFFILIATE LINK
→ MERCHANT CLICK
→ QUALIFYING PURCHASE
→ TRACKED COMMISSION
→ VALIDATED COMMISSION
→ SETTLED CASH
```

**Primary first test market: United States, English.**  
Reason is not a claim that the US is universally “best”; it is a **test-selection assumption** based on broad merchant/network depth, English production simplicity, Amazon/Awin/impact availability, and low localization overhead. It must be validated with real click/conversion evidence before scale.

### 3.1 Amazon Associates

**VERIFIED**
- Amazon Associates supports content creators/publishers and provides customized linking tools; qualifying purchases/actions can generate commission.
- The Amazon Associates country selector currently lists, among others: United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, Canada, Brazil, Australia, UAE, Singapore and Netherlands.
- Amazon OneLink documentation supports international earning across Canada, EU5 (UK, Spain, Germany, France, Italy) and additional marketplaces including Japan, Singapore, Netherlands and Australia.
- Amazon.com says commission income is paid approximately **60 days after the end of the month** in which it was earned.
- Amazon.com direct deposit/gift-card threshold is **$10**; check minimum is $100.
- Amazon.co.uk direct deposit/gift-card threshold is **£25**.
- Amazon.de direct deposit/gift-card threshold is **€25**.
- Amazon US application review requires **three qualifying sales**; applicants have **180 days** to refer a sale and may be withdrawn if qualifying-sales requirements are not met.
- Affiliate disclosure is mandatory; Amazon requires clear disclosure near affiliate links and the account/site identification statement required by its Operating Agreement.

### 3.2 Awin

**VERIFIED**

### 3.4 YouTube native monetization

**VERIFIED**
- As of access date, expanded YPP early access requires **500 subscribers**, 3 valid public uploads in 90 days, plus either **3,000 qualified watch hours** in the prior 12 months or **3 million qualified Shorts views** in the prior 90 days.
- Ad/Premium revenue sharing requires **1,000 subscribers** plus either 4,000 qualified watch hours or 10 million qualified public Shorts views under the current pre-Feb-2027 thresholds.
- YouTube has announced higher ads/Premium entry thresholds beginning **2027-02-01** for new applicants; therefore no 2026 model should hard-code current thresholds as permanent.
- YouTube states that repetitive/mass-produced, templated or minimally varied content can be ineligible for monetization.
- YouTube’s reused-content policy is **separate from copyright enforcement**: having permission from an original creator does not by itself guarantee YPP eligibility.

- Eligible videos begin collecting rewards after reaching **1,000 qualified For You views**, but only after creator/program eligibility is met.

## 9. Time-to-first-revenue vs time-to-settled-cash

MCME must store these as separate states:

```text
CLICK
→ TRACKED_EVENT
→ PENDING_REVENUE
→ APPROVED_REVENUE
→ PAYABLE
→ SETTLED_CASH
```

**Do not collapse them.**

Examples:
- Amazon can record qualifying commission well before cash because payout is approximately 60 days after month-end.
- Awin can show pending/approved/declined transactions and pays only after validation/threshold/payment-cycle conditions.
- impact.com can show partner earnings before payout, but withdrawal requires balance and payment prerequisites.

**North-star for first experiment:** first real **SETTLED_CASH**, with intermediate states retained as evidence.

## 10. Originality / copyright / policy rule

**VERIFIED policy distinction:**
- Copyright permission asks whether MCME has the legal right to use material.
- Platform monetization eligibility asks whether the resulting channel/content satisfies that platform’s originality/authenticity/reused-content rules.
- One does **not** imply the other.

Therefore:

```text
RIGHTS PASS ≠ MONETIZATION PASS
MONETIZATION PASS ≠ RIGHTS PASS
BOTH MUST PASS
```

MCME should import **ideas, facts, formats and market signals**, not copy third-party finished videos as the business model.
