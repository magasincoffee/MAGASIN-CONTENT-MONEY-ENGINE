# GLOBAL MARKET SELECTION V1

Research snapshot: 2026-09-19

## Principle

There is no permanent “best country.”

The best target is the current market/platform/niche combination with the highest expected risk-adjusted net cash contribution and acceptable policy/operational risk.

## Market score

For candidate market m:

```text
Expected Cash Score =
  Revenue Potential
× Monetization Coverage
× Purchase Intent
× Conversion Feasibility
× Distribution Opportunity
× Payout Feasibility
÷ (Competition + Localization Cost + Production Cost + Policy Risk)
```

Weights must eventually be learned from our own data.

## Required signals

- platform eligibility;
- platform earnings-rate/RPM evidence when available;
- affiliate EPC and commission;
- conversion rate;
- average order value;
- advertiser demand proxy;
- purchasing power;
- social usage;
- search demand;
- product availability;
- payout/KYC feasibility;
- language production cost;
- competition;
- content originality burden;
- policy/copyright risk.

## Current platform evidence

### YouTube
YouTube Partner Program is available across a broad set of countries including United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, UAE, Brazil and Vietnam.

YouTube monetization requires original/authentic content. Reused content without significant original commentary, substantive modification or added educational/entertainment value can make a channel ineligible.

### TikTok Creator Rewards
Current official TikTok support references program terms for:
- Brazil
- France
- Germany
- Japan
- South Korea
- United Kingdom
- United States

This makes these seven markets especially important candidates when platform rewards are part of the money loop.

### Facebook
Facebook Content Monetization pays across eligible formats and remains invite-based. Meta reported nearly USD 3B paid to creators during 2025 and is explicitly prioritizing original content while deprioritizing duplicative/minor-edit content.

### Amazon Associates
Amazon operates country-specific Associates programs across major markets in the Americas, Europe and Asia-Pacific, creating an affiliate path independent of platform ad monetization.

These may produce lower platform revenue per qualified view in some niches but can win on audience scale, production/localization economics, affiliate products or lead value.

## Important limitation

Platform RPM/CPM is not a fixed country constant and public cross-country numbers are often third-party estimates.

Therefore the Robot must not hard-code “US RPM = X” as truth.

The system will start with market priors, then replace them with:
- our actual qualified views;
- actual earnings;
- actual clicks;
- actual EPC;
- actual conversion;
- actual settled cash.

## First hypothesis

English/United States is a strong first research candidate because one content asset can access a large advertiser/affiliate ecosystem and English can be reused across multiple markets.

This is a hypothesis to test, not an irreversible decision.
