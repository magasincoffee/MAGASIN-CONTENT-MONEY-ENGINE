# PROFIT + CASH METRICS

## North Star

NET CASH CONTRIBUTION PER 1,000 QUALIFIED VIEWS

```text
NCC_1K =
(
 settled platform revenue
+ settled affiliate commission
+ attributable lead/product contribution
+ sponsor revenue allocation
- variable content production cost
- voice/video/tool cost
- media spend
- transaction/refund cost
) / qualified_views × 1000
```

## Why qualified views

Raw views can include traffic that:
- cannot monetize;
- has no purchasing intent;
- comes from unsupported markets;
- is invalid/non-qualified;
- is disconnected from the target monetization event.

## Core experiment metrics

### Attention
- impressions;
- qualified views;
- view-through rate;
- retention/watch time.

### Intent
- profile/link CTR;
- affiliate CTR;
- search uplift;
- lead-start rate.

### Transaction
- conversion rate;
- AOV;
- EPC;
- commission;
- lead value;
- product contribution margin.

### Cost
- research cost;
- voice cost;
- video/render cost;
- tooling/API cost;
- publishing cost;
- paid distribution.

### Cash
- gross earned;
- pending;
- approved;
- settled;
- refunded/reversed;
- net cash.

## Decision metrics

KEEP:
evidence suggests positive expected contribution and more learning has value.

KILL:
expected contribution is negative/weak and no cheap discriminating test remains.

SCALE:
positive economics replicated with sufficient confidence and no unresolved policy/operations constraint.

## Anti-vanity rule

Followers, likes and total views may be diagnostic signals but never the final business objective.
