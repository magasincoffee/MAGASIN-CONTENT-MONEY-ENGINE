# SOT — MISSION & ECONOMICS

## Mission

Convert global social trend signals into compliant Vietnamese Facebook traffic and Shopee Affiliate cash with minimum Owner involvement.

## Primary objective

FIRST REAL CASH as soon as practical, then repeatable net cash.

## North Star

NET CASH CONTRIBUTION / 1,000 QUALIFIED VIEWS

## Evidence ladder

L0 — no qualified commercial signal
L1 — verified affiliate click
L2 — attributed order
L3 — approved commission
L4 — cash received and reconciled, with measured direct cost/time

## Cash invariant

view != click != order != pending commission != approved commission != payout != cash received

Never report revenue/cash using an earlier stage.

## Cost model

Track:
- software/API spend;
- paid media if ever approved;
- contractor/licensing spend if any;
- compute/render cost;
- estimated human exception time.

## Decision priority

Real cash > attributed order > affiliate click > qualified attention > raw views.

A high-view creative with poor commercial efficiency can lose to a lower-view creative with stronger cash contribution.
