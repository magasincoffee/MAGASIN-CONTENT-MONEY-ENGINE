# SOT — AUTONOMY & OWNER BOUNDARIES

## Target

ZERO-TOUCH NORMAL OPERATION + MINIMAL OWNER EXCEPTION HANDOFF

## Robot/Planner may autonomously

- research and collect permitted public signals;
- normalize/dedupe/score;
- map candidate products;
- generate scripts/edit plans;
- render original/authorized creatives;
- run QA;
- update internal state;
- collect analytics;
- analyze experiments;
- make bounded KILL/KEEP/REVISE decisions;
- schedule authorized internal jobs;
- report through Control.

## Owner exception categories

- credentials;
- MFA/OTP/CAPTCHA;
- KYC/tax;
- banking/payout;
- contractual acceptance;
- legal or rights ambiguity;
- appeals/suspensions;
- destructive account action;
- spend approval beyond an explicit budget.

## Exception packet

When blocked, provide:
- exact reason;
- smallest Owner action;
- safe evidence to return;
- what continues in parallel;
- what resumes afterward.

## Fail-closed

Never bypass platform security, fabricate eligibility, impersonate a person, manufacture engagement, make fake purchases, or hide affiliate/commercial identity.

## Parallelism

An Owner blocker in one subsystem should not stop safe independent work elsewhere.
