# SOT — RIGHTS, POLICY & TRANSFORMATION

## Core rule

Trend/reference access is not publication permission.

## Rights classes

PASS:
- original;
- licensed;
- creator-authorized;
- supplier/merchant-authorized;
- platform-permitted for the intended use;
- public-domain;
- otherwise documented rights-verified.

REFERENCE_ONLY:
may inform analysis, hook, angle, pacing, topic, product, scene concept, or script; may not be republished as an asset.

BLOCK:
unknown or conflicting rights where publication depends on that material.

## Transformation standard

The system should add substantive new value through combinations such as:
- new Vietnamese script/commentary;
- original voice/narration;
- new analysis or explanation;
- new demonstration;
- new scene plan;
- new graphics/data;
- meaningful story restructure;
- new comparison or decision support.

Crop, border, speed change, subtitle-only, watermark removal, or trivial edits are not a sufficient transformation standard.

## Safety/claims

Do not invent efficacy, health, safety, savings, scarcity, pricing, discount, seller, or product-performance claims.

## Runtime gate

No asset reaches publish-ready state without rights_status and policy_status.

UNKNOWN != PASS.
