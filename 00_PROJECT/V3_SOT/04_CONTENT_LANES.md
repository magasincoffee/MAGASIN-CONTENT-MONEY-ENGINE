# SOT — CONTENT LANES

## Definition

A content lane is a bounded audience/content hypothesis within the primary Fanpage.

Each lane requires:
- lane_id;
- audience promise;
- topic boundary;
- hook families;
- likely product classes;
- commercial hypothesis;
- primary metric;
- stop condition;
- status: TEST / KEEP / SCALE / REVISE / KILL.

## Initial policy

Run few lanes at a time. Do not create a random-content page.

A lane can be broad enough for discovery but must remain understandable to the audience.

## Lane scoring

Evaluate:
- qualified reach;
- retention;
- shares/comments quality;
- affiliate CTR;
- attributed order rate;
- approved commission per 1,000 qualified views;
- production difficulty;
- rights/policy risk.

## Lifecycle

IDEA -> TEST -> KEEP or REVISE or KILL -> SCALE only with commercial evidence.

## Kill law

A lane that repeatedly produces attention without commercial signal must be revised or killed rather than protected by vanity metrics.

## Expansion law

New lanes enter only through a named hypothesis and bounded experiment.
