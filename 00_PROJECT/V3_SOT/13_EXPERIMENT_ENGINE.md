# SOT — EXPERIMENT ENGINE

## Unit of learning

An experiment tests a named change in one or more of:
source, lane, topic, hook, structure, creative, product, CTA, schedule, or audience framing.

## Required fields

experiment_id, hypothesis, baseline, variants, controlled variable, start/end, minimum observation rule, primary metric, guardrails, result, decision, evidence refs.

## Decisions

KILL — insufficient value; stop.
KEEP — worth further learning; no scaling claim.
REVISE — signal exists but bottleneck is identifiable.
SCALE — allocate more resources only with evidence strong enough for the tested dimension.

## Commercial priority

For product/content scaling, prefer L2+ evidence where possible.

L1 click evidence can justify continued learning but not a claim of profitable scale.

## Portfolio rule

Capacity moves from weak experiments to stronger evidence-backed experiments.

## Anti-overfit

Do not declare a winner from trivial samples. Small samples can trigger a new test, not fake certainty.

## Five-Step

Each experiment must show which requirement was questioned, what was deleted, how the test was simplified, how cycle time was accelerated, and what automation became justified.
