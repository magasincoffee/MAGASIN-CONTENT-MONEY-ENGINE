- [ ] A unique affiliate tracking link can be generated.

- [ ] The real program shows the validation/locking logic.

- [ ] Owner-authorized KYC/tax/payment steps are known.
- [ ] A feasible bank/PayPal/Payoneer or other permitted payout rail exists.

- [ ] Required commercial/affiliate disclosure is identified.

# 6. Smallest justified Gate B batch

## Minimum batch = **3 static Pins**

Exactly:

```text
3 × Static 2:3 original problem-solution checklist Pins
1 × selected sub-problem
1 × qualified merchant path
1 × money model
0 × paid traffic
0 × video
0 × voice
```

## Why 3 is the minimum

**One Pin is too weak**:
- zero distribution could be a single-creative indexing/relevance failure;
- zero clicks could be one bad framing;
- a click could be one-off noise.

**Two Pins still leave a binary ambiguity**:
- if one gets distribution and one does not, there is no minimal third observation to distinguish a route-level failure from a creative-specific failure.

**Three Pins are the smallest batch that allows:**

1. the same sub-problem and merchant path to remain fixed;
2. three distinct buyer-intent framings;
3. a structural distinction between:
   - no distribution across the batch;
   - distribution but no outbound click across the batch;
   - at least one genuine outbound click;
   - at least one merchant action / commission event.

No 30-Pin batch is justified before the first commercial signal.

## Three planned framings — concepts only, not production

1. **Measure Before You Buy**  
   Fridge shelf width / depth / height checklist.

2. **Which Bin Shape Fits This Shelf?**  
   Original selection grid based on narrow/deep/tall shelf geometry.

3. **Fridge Fit Mistakes to Check Before Ordering**  
   Clearance / drawer / door / shelf-obstruction checklist.

The three Pins must use original schematic graphics; no production creative is generated in MCME-004.

---

## H3 — DISTRIBUTED CONTENT CAN PRODUCE A QUALIFIED OUTBOUND CLICK

**Hypothesis:** If the batch receives distribution, at least one genuine user will leave Pinterest through the qualified affiliate destination.

**Observable events:**
- Pinterest Outbound clicks;
- network click record where available;
- Pin-level tracking ID/sub-ID if supported.

Pinterest defines Outbound clicks as actions leading to a destination off Pinterest. [S8][S9]

**PASS:**
- at least one genuine, non-Owner outbound click occurs on a Pin linked to the qualified merchant path, preferably corroborated by network click reporting.

**FAIL:**
- the bounded batch has organic distribution but zero qualified outbound clicks by the observation deadline.

**Action if FAIL:** KILL/REVISE creative/CTA/problem framing; do not SCALE.

No CTR target is invented.

---

## H4 — A QUALIFIED CLICK CAN BECOME ATTRIBUTED MONEY

**Hypothesis:** The qualified merchant path can turn a genuine click into an observable commission state.

**Observable events:**
- tracked merchant action;
- pending commission;
- validated commission;
- payable commission;
- settled payout/cash.

**PASS for learning:**
- any genuine tracked merchant action appears.

**PASS for revenue proof:**
- a pending commission is attributed to MCME traffic.

**PASS for validation proof:**
- that commission becomes validated/approved.

**PASS for cash proof:**
- the related payout settles to the Owner-authorized rail.

**FAIL:**
- qualified clicks exist but no merchant action is ever attributed within the real program's applicable attribution/validation window.

**Action if FAIL:** investigate offer/landing-page/intent mismatch before changing the route.

No conversion-rate or revenue target is invented.

## Gate B — merchant action / commission

### KEEP STRONGER

If:
- a genuine tracked merchant action appears;

then:
- retain the offer/content path for validation observation.

Scale consideration begins only after **conversion/commission evidence exists**.

```text
tracked merchant action
AND
attributed commission state
```

Views, impressions, saves, Pin clicks or outbound clicks alone are never enough.

### CASH PROOF

The strongest proof is:

```text
validated commission
→ payable commission
→ settled cash
```

No intermediate state is renamed "cash."

# 17. Commercial-signal definitions

These definitions are canonical for the experiment.

## FIRST COMMERCIAL SIGNAL

The first **genuine qualified outbound click** from one of the bounded experiment Pins to the Gate-A-qualified affiliate merchant path.

Operational truth:

- Pinterest records an Outbound click to the destination URL; [S8][S9]
- if network click reporting is available, the click should be corroborated there;
- Owner/Work/Robot-generated test clicks are excluded.

This is **intent evidence**, not revenue.

---

## FIRST ATTRIBUTED REVENUE

The first network-recorded commission-bearing merchant action attributable to an experiment tracking link, even if the commission is still pending.

```text
tracked merchant action
→ pending attributed commission
```

This is revenue attribution evidence, not validated revenue and not cash.

---

## FIRST VALIDATED REVENUE

The first attributed commission that survives the network/merchant validation process and enters the network's validated/approved state.

```text
pending commission
→ validated / approved commission
```

This is stronger than pending revenue, but still not cash.

---

## FIRST SETTLED CASH

The first payout amount attributable to the experiment that is actually received through the Owner-authorized payout rail and can be reconciled to the related payable commission/payout record.

```text
validated commission
→ payable commission
→ payout issued
→ funds received
= FIRST SETTLED CASH
```

Anything before funds received is **not settled cash**.
